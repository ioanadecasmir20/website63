About Talon Group

Calm, professional security built around the assignment.

London-based protection with an emphasis on discretion, planning and clear communication.

Our approach

Security should support the client, not dominate the environment.

Talon Group has been positioned as a London-focused private security provider with close protection at the centre of its service offering. Our operational approach is based on understanding context before deciding what level of protection is appropriate.

Assignments are planned around the client’s movements, locations, schedule, privacy expectations and specific concerns. Where additional services are required, close protection can be integrated with residential security, guarding, event support, keyholding and protective transport.

Our principles

How Talon Group approaches protection.

01

Discretion

Protection should be effective without unnecessarily drawing attention to the client.

02

Proportion

The security posture should reflect the actual environment and level of risk.

03

Preparation

Good protection relies on planning, communication and understanding the itinerary.

04

Professionalism

Clear conduct, presentation and communication are central to every assignment.
